Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

ClipBot rclone is a personal app used only by its owner, JonnySS. It uses the rclone tool to copy video clips from the owner's computer into the owner's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. That means it can only see and change files and folders that this app created itself. It cannot read anything else in Google Drive.

What it collects

Nothing. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The sign-in token stays on the owner's own computer.
